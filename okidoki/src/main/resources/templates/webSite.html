About Us

Oki Doki Global is a digitally powered transport solutions provider backed by its parent company, Expolanka Holdings, a leading conglomerate specializing in logistics, leisure, and investment.

Expolanka Holdings’ freight cluster, EFL Global, is a leader in freight forwarding and logistics, with a presence in 39 countries.

Our Mission

To be the most admired and innovative logistics solutions provider, delivering excellence through technology, customer-centricity, and sustainable practices.

Our Vision

To revolutionize the logistics industry by leveraging cutting-edge technology and fostering a culture of continuous improvement, ensuring seamless and efficient supply chain solutions for our global clientele.

Our Team

At the heart of our logistics and transport management success is a team of dedicated professionals who bring expertise, passion, and reliability to every shipment. From skilled drivers and logistics coordinators to customer support specialists and operations managers, each member of our team is committed to ensuring smooth, efficient, and timely deliveries.
